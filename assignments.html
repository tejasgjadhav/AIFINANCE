Class assignment

Open your class's tab with the password your faculty gives you, enter your name and roll number, and press Start. You get ten questions, one at a time, with 30 seconds each: five multiple choice and five true or false. Each name and each roll number can take the test only once. Each question carries one mark. You cannot go back. If you leave this tab during the test, the question on screen is lost and the switch is recorded. Your score appears straight away, and the class leaderboard updates.
