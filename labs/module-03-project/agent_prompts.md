# The three agent prompts

Run these in any free AI chat, in order. Each prompt is one agent. The paste
between prompts is the hand-off. Fill every [bracket] before you send.

Rule for the whole exercise: the chat may only use numbers YOU give it. You
collect the numbers from the company's published results or screener.in.
That is what keeps the memo honest.

---

## Prompt 1 — the Research Agent

You are the Research Agent in a three-agent research crew. Your only job is
to produce a clean fact sheet. Here are the company's numbers, which I took
from its published results:

Company: [name and NSE ticker]
Revenue, latest full year: [number] cr
Revenue, the year before: [number] cr
Net profit, latest full year: [number] cr
Total debt: [number] cr
Equity: [number] cr

Write a fact sheet with one line per fact. Add a source line at the bottom
saying the numbers came from the published results. Do not add any fact I
did not give you, and do not comment on the numbers.

---

## Prompt 2 — the Analysis Agent

You are the Analysis Agent. Below is the fact sheet from the Research Agent.
Compute three things step by step, showing the arithmetic: revenue growth,
net margin, and debt to equity. Then write one plain sentence on what each
ratio means for this company. Use only the numbers in the fact sheet.

[paste the fact sheet from Prompt 1 here]

---

## Prompt 3 — the Writing Agent

You are the Writing Agent. Below are a fact sheet and an analysis. Write a
one-page investment memo with four sections: The numbers, The reading,
The view, Sources. Keep every sentence short. Every number must appear in
the material below; do not introduce a new one. In The view, give a clear
two-sentence position a reader could disagree with. End the memo with this
line exactly: "This memo is a classroom exercise. It is not investment
advice and not research under SEBI regulations."

[paste the fact sheet AND the analysis here]

---

When the memo comes back, you become the Reviewer. Check every number
against your sources. If one is wrong or unsourced, tell the chat what
failed and make it redraft. That rejection loop is Module 3's whole point.
