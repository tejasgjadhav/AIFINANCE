Live walkthrough: corporate login and AI at work

The instructor shows how staff at a large financial firm sign in, and how they use AI tools in their daily work. You watch, ask questions and take notes. There is nothing to install and nothing to build.

A  corporate login  →  B  AI at work  →  C  your notes
The firm is not named. Its name is not on any slide, page or note. Please do not photograph or record the screen. Write down the controls you see, not the brand.

ACorporate login30 minutes

  • The company ID. Staff sign in with one company account. It opens email, chat and the AI tools.
  • The second check. A code or approval on the phone confirms it is really the employee.
  • The leaver. When someone leaves, the account is switched off the same day, and every tool closes with it.
  • No personal email. Work data never goes into a personal account or a personal AI chat.

BAI at work45 minutes

  • The approved tools. Which AI tools staff are allowed to use, and how they open them.
  • Daily use. Drafting an email, summarising a long document, and help with code.
  • The data rules. What may go into a prompt, and what may not.
  • The human check. Where a person reads, corrects and signs before anything is used.

CYour notes15 minutes

Write one line against each question while you watch.
