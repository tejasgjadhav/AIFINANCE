Live walkthrough: corporate login and AI at work

The instructor shows how staff at a large financial firm sign in and use AI tools in a normal working day. You watch, ask questions and take notes. There is nothing to install and nothing to build.

A  corporate login  →  B  AI at work  →  C  your notes
The firm is not named. Its name is not on any slide, page or note. Please do not photograph or record the screen. Write down how the tools are used, not the brand.

ACorporate login30 minutes

  • The company ID. Staff sign in with one company account. It opens email, chat and the AI tools.
  • The AI tools. Which AI tools open once the staff member is signed in.
  • Where the AI sits. Inside email, team chat and documents, not in a separate website.
  • Starting a task. How a staff member asks the AI for help in the middle of normal work.

BAI at work45 minutes

  • Drafting. Writing a first draft of an email or a short note.
  • Summarising. Turning a long document or a meeting into a few key points.
  • Searching. Finding the right company document by asking a question in plain words.
  • Coding help. Getting help to write or fix a small piece of code.

CYour notes15 minutes

Write one line against each question while you watch.

#Question
1How does a staff member sign in, and which AI tools open after that?
2Which everyday tasks did the AI help with?
3What did the staff member type to ask for help?
4Which task did AI make faster, and by roughly how much?
5Where did the staff member still have to check or correct the answer?
6Name one task from your own work or studies you would use it for.
